2. Vault

Choose a different vault, or create one

Deploying creates a new vault with a new address. If your team already has a vault, connect to it above instead: money sent to a new vault is not in the old one.

Is your money protected?

The vault protects only the USDC inside it. USDC left in the agent's wallet, or in any wallet the agent controls, is covered by no rule: the agent can send it anywhere. Move your funds into the vault and leave the agent's address with gas only.
