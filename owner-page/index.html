Styx Arc testnet. Unaudited.

A vault that pays an agent's invoices within your rules

Styx holds USDC in a vault on Arc testnet. An AI agent can pay routine invoices up to the limits you set. For anything above a limit, the vault needs a signature from you or from an approver, over the exact payment. A reviewer can check one disclosed record against the chain.

Testnet only, and not audited. Approvers sign from a plain wallet; a smart-contract wallet such as a Safe cannot approve through these pages.

This page is where you review and sign an exception. You normally arrive here from an alert link that carries the request, so there is nothing to sign yet.
