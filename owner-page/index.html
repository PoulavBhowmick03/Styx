01The rules

The agent can pay. It cannot change the rules.

Limits, recipients, approvers, pause and withdraw are all owner-only in the contract. The transcript below illustrates the rules; it is not a recording.

02How it works

Four steps, each one checkable on the chain.

  1. Fund the vault

    You put USDC in a vault you own, and set a per-payment limit, a daily limit and the vendors it may pay.

  2. The agent pays

    Routine payments within your limits go through on the agent's own request. The agent's key holds gas only.

  3. You sign exceptions

    Over a limit, the vault waits. You get an alert, review the recipient and amount here, and sign that exact payment.

  4. A reviewer checks

    One disclosed record, checked against the chain with the reviewer's own RPC. No other record is readable.

03Who can do what

Three keys, three jobs.

Owner

Sets the rules.

Can

  • Set limits and allow vendors
  • Sign exceptions, pause, withdraw

Agent

Pays within them.

Can

  • Propose and submit allowed payments

Cannot

  • Change limits, recipients or approvers
  • Pause, sign exceptions or withdraw

Reviewer

Checks one record.

Can

  • Open one disclosed record
  • Verify it against Arc

Cannot

  • Read any other record
