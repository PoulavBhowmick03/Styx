02How it works

Four steps, each one checkable on the chain.

  1. Fund the vault

    The owner funds a vault on NEAR, allows the recipients it may pay, and sets each agent's limits per payment and per day.

  2. The agent pays

    Within the limits it pays on its own, on NEAR or, through Chain Signatures, in USDC on Arc and Base from the vault's one EVM address.

  3. The approver signs

    Over a limit, the request arrives in the approver's own Telegram chat. They review the payment, read back from the vault, and sign that exact payment in their NEAR wallet.

  4. A reviewer checks

    Each payment can carry a record sealed to one reviewer, who opens it with their own key and checks it against the chain with their own RPC.

03Who can do what

Four keys, four jobs.

Owner

Sets the rules.

Can

  • Set limits, recipients, approvers and EVM chains
  • Pause and withdraw

Approver

Signs exceptions.

Can

  • Authorize one payment over the limit
  • Get each request in their own Telegram

Cannot

  • Change the rules

Agent

Pays within them.

Can

  • Pay allowed recipients within the limits

Cannot

  • Change limits, recipients or approvers
  • Approve, pause or withdraw

Reviewer

Checks one record.

Can

  • Open one sealed record
  • Verify it against NEAR

Cannot

  • Read any other record
