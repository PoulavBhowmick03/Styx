How sentinels learn

Each sentinel is scored: a pause during a real attack counts for it, a false alarm counts twice against it, a missed attack once. At minus two it is retired and a new generation takes over, a little looser after false alarms or a little stricter after misses. Nothing changes on the vault until the owner approves it.
