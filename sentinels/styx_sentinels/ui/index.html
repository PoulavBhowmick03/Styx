SentinelsWatching a vault

Watchers that can only hit the brakes.

Each sentinel reads the vault's payments and may pause it. NEAR lets a sentinel's key do nothing else: no paying, no unpausing, no changing the rules. A parent scores them, retires the noisy ones and spawns their next generation.

The swarm

Payments and pauses

Bars are payments, taller for larger amounts; ember bars went to a recipient a sentinel flagged. An ember line is a sentinel's pause, a green line the owner's unpause. A shaded band is a marked attack.

    How a sentinel is scored

    A pause during a marked attack is a catch. A pause the owner reverses with no attack marked is a false alarm. The score is catches minus two times false alarms. At minus two the parent retires the sentinel, asks the owner to remove its key, and spawns the next generation with what it learned.
