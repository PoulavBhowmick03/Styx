SentinelsWatching a vault

Watchers that can only hit the brakes.

Each sentinel reads the vault's payments and holds a NEAR key that can call one method: sentinel_pause. The parent keeps the fittest, retires the rest, and breeds their next generation. The owner ratifies every change on chain.

Payments and pauses

bars are payments; shaded spans are marked attacks

Evolution

at fitness -2 a sentinel is retired and its child mutates

Looser after false alarms, stricter after misses: one mutation per generation, with a note the child inherits. A sentinel's energy is the gas allowance left on its NEAR key; when it runs low the parent proposes a fresh key, same genome.
